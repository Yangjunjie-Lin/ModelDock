# Marketplace supplier policy

Policy version: `marketplace-launch-2026-08-21`

## Eligibility

A supplier must provide a verifiable legal entity, registration and tax information, legal/primary/billing/security contacts, controlled endpoint, approved data-residency declaration, approved security questionnaire, active contract, approved resale permission, and an encrypted payout destination. KYB, endpoint ownership, endpoint isolation, model, price, region, and security evidence are independently reviewed.

All Provider connections must have an explicit contract status, allowed customer and processing regions, prohibited regions, and a disabled/kill switch. Default or missing production authorization fails closed.

## Required conduct

The supplier must use official credentials and contract-permitted access, honor rate and cost limits, keep tenant data isolated, disclose subprocessors and retention, report ownership or security changes, and keep prices and model capabilities current. It must not train on customer content unless the customer and ModelDock have expressly authorized that use.

## Prohibited conduct

The Marketplace does not permit account farming or bulk registration, CAPTCHA or verification bypass, proxy or region-evasion, credential/API-key sale or transfer, scraping credentials, false legal identities, concealment of subprocessors, manipulation of probes, fabricated usage, or any behavior that violates a Provider/model contract or law.

Supplier declarations cannot mutate platform quality policy, routing grades, billing usage, wallet transactions, payable accruals, or payout approval.

## Enforcement

ModelDock may request evidence, restrict a model or region, reduce traffic, suspend the supplier, open the circuit, reject a price, hold settlement, or terminate access. Emergency cutover is immediate when continued routing could harm customers, data, funds, or legal compliance. Every action records an operator reason and immutable lifecycle/audit evidence.

Exit stops new traffic and ends Provider links. It does not delete financial, tax, security, dispute, or audit records, and it does not turn supplier-submitted usage into payable usage.
